\title{Near-Lossless Knowledge Distillation:\\
Definitions, Error Decompositions, Capacity Lower Bounds and Objective Design}
\author{A theoretical treatment}
\date{September 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We give a mathematically explicit account of distilling a teacher $p_{\TT}(y\mid x)$ into a smaller
autoregressive student $q_\theta(y\mid x)$. We (i)~formalise ``lossless'' through divergences between
sequence-level laws under a deployment input distribution and prove the relations among forward KL,
reverse KL, total variation, $\JSD_\beta$, Hellinger and task-level excess risk, including exact
chain-rule identities that pair each KL direction with a specific prefix distribution;
(ii)~decompose the student's gap into approximation, estimation, optimisation, prefix-shift (exposure
bias) and covariate-shift terms and bound each, recovering the $O(\eps T)$ versus $O(\eps T^2)$
compounding dichotomy and showing exactly when on-policy training removes it. The recoverability constant
that governs on-policy compounding is sharp. It can be replaced by its average over the states where the
student errs, but not by its average over the teacher's own prefixes. It is at most one plus twice the mixing
time of a cost-sufficient latent chain of the teacher; (iii)~prove
information-theoretic (rate--distortion) lower bounds on the achievable divergence as a function of
the student's description length, together with a softmax-bottleneck rank bound, and derive the
power-law forms they predict; (iv)~analyse the logit gradients of the standard objectives, the
role of temperature, and the Fisher-information content of soft labels, and propose a
sequence-level, prefix-adaptive $\JSD_\beta$ objective with proven exactness, boundedness,
agnostic total-variation competitiveness, consistency, and an unbiased Rao--Blackwellised gradient
estimator; (v)~analyse intermediate-representation matching, proving when it is redundant,
when it helps (noisy supervision, conditioning, compositional hardness) and when it over-constrains,
and propose a Fisher-aligned variant with no approximation cost in a linear-Gaussian model;
(vi)~assemble an end-to-end bound, separate what is proved from what is conjectured, and specify
experiments and open problems. Every inequality marked \emph{numerically checked} is verified by the
scripts in \texttt{verification/}.
\end{abstract}
